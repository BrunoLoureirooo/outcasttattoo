# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Portuguese adults, 18–36, skewing 70% male / 30% female. Tattoo collectors and first-timers based in and around Leira, Portugal. They follow tattoo artists on Instagram, know what blackwork and fine-line mean, and arrive with references already in hand. They're not buying a service — they're choosing an artist to mark their body permanently. The decision is emotional and identity-driven. They need to feel the shop's personality before they book.

## Product Purpose

Outcast Tattoo & Piercing is a custom street shop in Leira. The website exists to do three things: establish who Outcast is before a visitor scrolls, showcase the five artists and their portfolios, and funnel serious clients toward a booking. Success means a visitor lands, feels the brand in under three seconds, finds an artist whose work speaks to them, and books.

## Brand Personality

Unapologetic. Deliberate. Dark.

The physical object: dark steel chains — cold, heavy, interlocking. Not decorative. Not threatening for its own sake. Just permanently, unambiguously itself.

Voice: confident and spare. No warmth as performance. No hustle. The shop doesn't need to convince you; if you get it, you get it.

## Anti-references

- Generic tattoo shop templates with bright photos on white backgrounds, colored CTAs, and stock smiles
- Clean minimal portfolios (Squarespace-style grids, generous whitespace, brand "story" prose)
- Colorful / maximalist flash-art shops with neon and rainbow palette
- Corporate / luxury wellness aesthetics (beige, sage, rounded corners, soft shadows)
- Tourist-facing "safe" dark themes that are dark in name only (charcoal cards, purple gradients, glassmorphism)

## Design Principles

1. **Intimidate before welcoming.** The landing page earns the visitor's trust by being exactly what it says it is — not by softening the entry. Visitors who stay are the right ones.
2. **Darkness as atmosphere, not decoration.** Black is not a background choice. It is the material the brand is made of. Every element sits inside the darkness, not on top of it.
3. **Monochrome discipline.** One accent (warm ivory) used with restraint. Color is not emphasis; contrast, weight, and scale are emphasis.
4. **Let the work speak.** Artist portfolios are the product. Typography and layout exist to frame the work, not compete with it.
5. **Weight over decoration.** Heavy type, deliberate spacing, structural motifs (thorns, chain geometry). No decorative flourish that doesn't earn its place.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA minimum (4.5:1 body text, 3:1 large/bold)
- Reduced motion support required — `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` on all animations including the SVG logo loader
- High contrast is inherent to the palette (near-white on true black exceeds WCAG AAA)
- Semantic HTML throughout — screen reader navigable
- No content gated behind JS or animation completion
